Connected agriculture · field insight

A clearer picture of growing conditions.

GreenGuardian brings on-site sensor readings, local weather, and crop recommendations together—helping make field conditions easier to understand at a glance.

From field signals to useful context.

A compact IoT workflow connects physical sensors to a live web experience.

01

Measure

An ESP32 collects temperature, humidity, rain, light, soil-moisture, and canopy-distance readings from connected sensors.

02

Enrich

A Flask server receives readings over the local network and combines them with weather and air-quality information from OpenWeather.

03

Explore

A scikit-learn model predicts a crop from climate features, while Gemini provides three additional crop options based on the broader context.

Model and data

Recommendations with context

The crop model uses temperature, humidity, and rainfall. Its training data is based on Atharva Ingle's Crop Recommendation Dataset, licensed under Apache 2.0. The project training data omits the N, P, K, and pH columns.

Rainfall is estimated from the current OpenWeather one-hour precipitation value multiplied by 24. Sensor labels use configurable starter thresholds. Recommendations are informational and are not a guarantee of crop performance.
